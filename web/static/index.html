kambience

Quiet sound for the places in your books: the rain on the roof, the fire in the inn, the sea against the hull. The sounds are written into the book itself, and play in KOReader when you turn them on.

I. Choose a book

    Once it's done you can download the copy with sound, or add it to the Calibre library.

    II. In progress

      Nothing yet. Choose a book above and it will appear here.

      Settings

      The AI that reads each book and decides where sounds belong. Any key stays on mattbot.

      Lower it to try a book out quickly, or to do a long book in parts: the rest stays silent, and pressing continue on that book later carries on from where it stopped.
