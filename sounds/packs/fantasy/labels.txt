# Fantasy pack: settings common in fantasy and historical novels (inns, roads, forges, villages).
# Download with: python3 fetch_sounds.py --pack fantasy
# Files land beside the core sounds (sounds/<label>.mp3), so the plugin needs nothing extra.

tavern/quiet-fire     Quiet inn common room at night: a fire burning in the hearth, very few patrons
road/wagon            Travelling by horse-drawn wagon or cart on a dirt road
road/horseback        Riding a horse along a country road or track
camp/travelers-night  Travellers' camp at night beside a road: campfire, crickets, open fields
village/farm          Farming village by day: chickens, livestock, rural quiet
village/festival      Village festival or fair outdoors: lively crowd, celebration
village/stable        Inside a stable: horses shifting and snorting, flies, straw
forge/smithy          Blacksmith's forge: hammer on anvil, fire, quenching
indoor/workshop       Busy craft workshop: hand tools, tinkering, wood and metal work
bells/town            Church or temple bells ringing over a town
city/rooftops-night   On city rooftops at night: wind, the town sleeping below
